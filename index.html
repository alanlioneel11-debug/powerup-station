    How it works

    Every kiosk has this QR code on the front. Scan it to check how long your phone will take before you pay.

    1. Scan the QR code and check your charge time.
    2. Choose a time and pay with card or cash.
    3. Set a 4-digit PIN, plug in and lock the door.
    4. Come back, type your PIN and grab your phone.
